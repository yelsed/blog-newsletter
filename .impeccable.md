## Design Context

### Users
Personal newsletter by one creator. The sole admin is a developer (Desley) — the public-facing site is for potential subscribers who share eclectic interests: tech, retro tech, webdesign, football, music, AI, poetry, life lessons.

### Brand Personality
**Playful · Expressive · Weird**  
Feels personal, assembled by a real human, not a design system. Like a laptop covered in stickers — eclectic topics coexist without needing to be coherent. No marketing speak. Honest and direct.

### Aesthetic Direction
"The Covered Laptop Lid": topic stickers scattered on a surface, 3D-tilting subscribe card as the central interaction. Light mode = warm desk/paper surface; dark mode = dark laptop lid plastic. Both driven by system preference.

Anti-references: Generic newsletter landing pages (Substack defaults, Mailchimp templates), glassmorphism, purple-to-blue gradients, any "AI slop" aesthetics.

References the user gave: laptop stickers as real design elements (not decorative clipart), cards that tilt/swivel on hover and respond to device orientation on mobile.

### Typography
- Display: **Bricolage Grotesque** — variable, organic widths, zine-assembled feel
- Body: **Figtree** — clean humanist sans with personality
- Both from Google Fonts

### Color Approach
Brand accent: `oklch(72% 0.22 135)` — chartreuse/lime green. Unexpected, energetic, not the AI defaults (cyan/purple/blue). Neutrals tinted toward accent hue with very low chroma. Full system light/dark.

### Design Principles
1. **Personal over polished** — feels made by one person, not a design agency
2. **Stickers are content, not decoration** — each represents a real newsletter topic
3. **Motion with purpose** — the 3D tilt is the memorable interaction; no other animation noise
4. **Unexpected accent color** — chartreuse breaks "safe tech = blue" and "personal = warm orange" reflexes
5. **Never generic** — if it could be on any newsletter's homepage, redesign it
